Timesheet

at Keka HR, 2022 as Senior Designer on Mobile app team


What is a Timesheet ?

A timesheet is a data table employers use to track employee hours worked over a period, typically for billing, payroll, compliance, and productivity analysis across tasks, projects, or clients.


What was my role ?

  • Collaborate with PM on Research & Design phase
  • Iterate low-fidelity & high-fidelity UI designs

Why was this required ?

  • Client Requirement
  • USP for US launch
  • Penetrate more on the service market

Why timesheet was needed on mobile app when the Web app was already there ?

  • Employees working on offsite- they have a problem with access hr.keka from remote locations. Current solution - external mobile app https://www.mywebtimesheets.com/
  • Clients laptops don’t allow users to access web app - makes keka app redundant

After the research phase I was laser focused on designing and PM took the backseat reviewing work daily fine tuning - copy, user journey flows of each personas.

We also prototyped few scenarios and gave the Link for folks internally to see if the solution would make sense.
